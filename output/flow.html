Asbestos Renovation & Demolition Notification Workflow

Follow the arrows from START. Blue diamonds are decisions, purple boxes are renovation submittals, red is demolition, green is completion.

Step-by-Step Guide

#StepWhat to do
1Check building & unitsSingle building with 4 or fewer residential units? Yes → Step 2. No → Step 3.
2Check Use CodeLook up the Broward County Property Appraiser Use Code. 01 → issue Certificate of Submittal (done). Not 01 → Step 4.
3Multiple small buildings?Renovating/demolishing more than one ≤4-unit residential building on the same site? Yes → Step 4. No → process does not apply; follow other requirements.
4Project typeRenovation → Step 5. Demolition → Step 9.
5Measure disturbed area≥160 sq ft walls/floors/ceilings OR ≥5,580 sq ft built-up roofing → Step 6. Less than both → Step 7.
6Large renovationSubmit asbestos survey certified by a Florida Licensed Asbestos Consultant + scope of work/demo plans → Step 8.
7Small renovationSubmit scope of work/demo plans proving the area is under the limits → Fee Table.
8Review survey≥160 sq ft of material with ≥1% asbestos? No → Fee Table. Yes → submit DEP Renovation Notice (Business Portal or PDF form) before starting, with scope, ownership, contractors, waste transporter, deposit site, start/finish dates and other required info → Fee Table.
9DemolitionBefore starting, submit a new asbestos survey (within 2 years) + DEP Notice of Demolition (Business Portal or PDF form) → Fee Table.

Notes

  • Fees must be paid before the submittal is reviewed.
  • Fees are subject to change by regulation.
  • All documents and notices are due BEFORE the project starts, unless noted otherwise.
  • Incomplete submittals may delay review.
  • This is a general guide; additional requirements may apply.
